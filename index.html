About Gary Waters

Gary Waters is the founder of Oak & Field Land Consultancy, drawing on experience across commercial forestry, woodland ecology, community forestry and agroforestry.

Based in Cumbria, Gary works with farmers, landowners, local authorities and environmental organisations to develop practical approaches to integrated land management.
